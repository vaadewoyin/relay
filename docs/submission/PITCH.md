# Finalist pitch preparation

Use this outline if invited to the December 1 online Discord pitch.

1. **Problem.** An operator arrives at a pump with an unresolved fault. Two shift reports describe the same test differently. Searching through reports and messages delays the point where someone can make a decision.
2. **Why G2.** An incoming operator needs the prioritized issue, both accounts, and one clear next action at the equipment. The glasses show those in short views navigated with deliberate gestures.
3. **Live product.** Show the real Notion source rows, then the official simulator shift brief, Gemini proposal, and exact citations. Acknowledge the finding, preview the handover, explicitly accept, and show the receipt and updated Notion owner.
4. **Differentiation and execution.** Relay closes the loop between reading a report and recording accountable work. Gemini proposes, deterministic code checks sources, and an operator authorizes. SQLite commits the receipt. External publication can be retried with the current owner and audit history preserved.
5. **Business potential and next proof.** Start with a facilities or hospitality shift team already using a shared register. Test time to find critical context and the share of handed-over work with a confirmed owner. Validate readability and interaction on physical G2 first.

| Likely question | Factual answer |
| --- | --- |
| Why glasses rather than a phone? | The narrow workflow helps at the equipment when the operator needs a glance and an action. Use the phone or Notion for long reports and administration. Physical ergonomics still need a pilot. |
| What does Relay read and write? | It reads the actual Notion report rows. After confirmation it can publish the verified receipt and write the current owner to the original stable Action Register row, with a separate ownership audit event. |
| Can the model transfer ownership? | No. It proposes a finding. Relay validates citations and the incoming operator explicitly confirms an eligible transfer against the current record versions. |
| What if Gemini or Notion is down? | Deterministic findings and the local workflow remain usable. A committed receipt stays verifiable and shows pending until publication is confirmed. |
| What do the AI numbers mean? | Synthetic data only. Original evaluation completed 30 of 40 cases, with precision 16/17 and end-to-end recall 16/19. A separate recovery completed 9 of 10 previously incomplete cases. A cited excerpt can still be misinterpreted. |
| How safe is the current integration? | It uses a dedicated synthetic workspace and trusts local demo actors on loopback. The Notion adapter checks remote edits, and concurrent writes still have a read/PATCH window. Shared deployment needs authenticated identities, scoped permissions, and physical validation. |
| Does voice or physical deployment work? | The verified workflow uses gestures in the official simulator. Speech input was unresolved on the tested WSLg build. Hardware installation and testing remain outstanding. |
